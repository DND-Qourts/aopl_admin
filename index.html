Give the team a 24-hour protected correction window.

—

The correction window is fixed at 24 hours. The team's current slot/queue priority remains protected during that window. If the deadline passes before resolution, the registration is not automatically cancelled, but the protected slot/priority is released.
Resolving inside the protected window restores the team's prior active review/waitlist position. If protection already expired, current division capacity and the team's fresh queue priority are used.
